## Stickman Kino

The full-body stick man poses `crouch`, `leap`, `tuck`, `hang`, `sit_edge`, `slump`, `sit_floor`, `relax`, `kneel`,
`lie`, `lie_front`, `point_up`, `cheer`, `hands_hips`, `arms_cross`, `surprise`, `scared`, `thumbs_up`, `salute`,
`bow`, `push`, `pull`, `carry`, `lift`, `windup`, `throw`, `balance`, `stumble`, `explain_open` and the moves `sneak`
and `climb_ladder` in `renderer/src/avatar/stickman.json` are converted from the pose table of Stickman Kino
(`engine/sm-rig.js`, https://github.com/dsauce/stickman-kino) by `work/kino_poses.py`.

The **Kino Line** body style (`kino` in `renderer/src/avatar/core.js`: proportions, side-view dot face), the hats and
hair `beanie`, `hardhat`, `tophat`, `grad_cap`, `chef_hat`, `beret`, `party_hat`, `bob_hair`, `bun`, `antenna`, the
square / filled heads, the `shirt` / `shorts` bands and the characters `zeke`, `office`, `exec`, `builder`,
`scientist`, `grad`, `chef`, `lady`, `robot`, `agent` and `ink_head` follow the figure drawing and character presets of
the same file.
